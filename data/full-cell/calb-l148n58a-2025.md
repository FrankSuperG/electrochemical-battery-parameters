## Tests and channels

Types: constant_current, cccv, pulse, dynamic, eis, ocv. Profiles: US06, UDDS, WLTP.

C/20 charge/discharge characterization; HPPC at 1C and C/3. Pulse rates are not a standalone multi-rate continuous-discharge sweep.

Reported temperature labels / degC: [10, 25, 40]. These are source conditions, not measured temperature channels.

Reported time-series channels: time, terminal_voltage, current, surface_temperature.

Reported frequency-domain channels: impedance.

Dataset description reports Arbin voltage/current and T-type surface thermocouples; Gamry EIS. Raw XLSX and processed MAT headers have not been independently checked.
